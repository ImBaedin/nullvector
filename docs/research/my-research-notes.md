- Research access uses the research site count model from [research-system-refined-plan.md](./research-system-refined-plan.md#6-research-network-size): each colony with a built Research Directorate contributes exactly 1 research site. The Research Directorate is a one-time facility with a maximum level of 1, so research access never sums facility levels or depends on multi-level Research Directorate upgrades.
- Other facility ships, defenses, buildings, etc. will no longer require a specific research facility level, but rather individual, specific unlockable research.
- The research tree will exist as part of the actual gameplay data package, so it won't need to be fetched, but we will need to fetch the players' levels/unlocks.
- I think it would be really neat if the tree had a specific layout and wasn't programmatically laid out. Because of this, I want to explore various designs of what the tree might look like. It should be fun to look at and explore. Additionally, dependencies should be easily visible.
- I think for the currency used for research, I want to explore having different tiers of some research point resource. These will be based on the difficulty of contracts that the player completes. Having these various tiers, something like common, rare, unobtainable, will force the player to interact with multiple systems of the game simultaneously without simply rushing to the highest tier contracts or to the highest tier research.
